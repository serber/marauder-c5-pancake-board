## Notes

- `J5`, `LED1` and `C14` are on the display side and are soldered by hand. All other parts are on the top side.
- `U6`: `ESP32-C5-WROOM-1U-N8R8` with or without `-V1.2` fits. Not `WROOM-1`, which has an on-board antenna and a longer body.
- `BT1`: footprint for Keystone 1042 (pad centres 78.7 mm, span 86.7 mm). Measure any other holder first.
- `U6`, `U2` and `Q2` were out of stock at LCSC on 2026-11-06. Stock of other parts was not checked.
- The `Device` column of `placement-rev1.csv` still names the old library part for passives changed on 2026-11-09. Fit the parts in this BOM.
