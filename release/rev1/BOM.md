## Substitutions

- `U6`: `ESP32-C5-WROOM-1U-N8R8` with or without `-V1.2` fits. Do not use `WROOM-1`, which has an on-board antenna and a longer body.
- `BT1`: the footprint is for Keystone 1042 (pad centers 78.7 mm, span 86.7 mm). Measure any other holder first.
- `C4`, `C5`, `C6`, `C12`, `C14`, `C16`, `C17`, `C19`: any 100 nF X7R 0603.

- Ceramic capacitors are all Samsung CL, chip resistors are all Yageo RC. Changed on 2026-11-09 from mixed YAGEO CC and UNI-ROYAL parts with the same values, footprints and ratings. 100 nF, 1k and 10k are the same parts as on `lmp91000-afe` v1.0. Most new resistors are JLCPCB Extended parts; the replaced UNI-ROYAL resistors were mostly Basic.
- `placement-rev1.csv` still names the old library device in its `Device` column for the changed parts. The `Manufacturer Part` and `Supplier Part` in `bom-rev1.csv` are the parts to fit.

Stock changes daily. The last check is in `docs/verification.md`. Stock and prices of the new passives were not checked.
